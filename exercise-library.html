Target
Chest
Equipment

Difficulty

Description

A fundamental compound pushing movement targeting the pectoral muscles, front deltoids, and triceps for upper body strength and muscle growth.

How to Perform

💡 Form Tip Keep shoulder blades retracted and feet firmly planted.
⚠️ Common Mistake Flaring elbows out to 90 degrees or bouncing the bar off the chest.
